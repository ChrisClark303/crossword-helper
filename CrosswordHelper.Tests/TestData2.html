This is an incomplete list of the most common abbreviations, shortenings and short synonyms often found in cryptics. Most are from the Chambers Dictionary, though a minority have been added as per vox populi usage. Please note that the criterion for entry into the main list is that it must be an abbreviation, shortening or synonym which is at most three letter and occurs with relative frequency in any of the major British cryptics.
